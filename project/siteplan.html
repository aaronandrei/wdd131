Site Plan

Site Name

Name: InCourage Club

The reason I chose InCourage Club for my site name is because it reflects the idea that I feel has helped a wide variety of people, including myself, when encouragement was needed to achieve the next big task. In courage is how we all progress and grow and we are in courage when we feel that support from others.

Optional domain availability: incourageclub.org

Site Purpose

The site will provide more information about the club's vision and provide inspiring stories of success with someone else's encouragement.
